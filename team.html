Meet the Team Behind ROID-AI

We are a 4-member undergraduate thesis team from the Polytechnic University of the Philippines, engineering deep learning architectures to automate genga-to-douga in-betweening for modern anime production.

Engineered ROI detection & deformable convolution layers
Architected the web UI & evaluation benchmark dashboards
Managed repository pipelines and model deployment interfaces

Authored methodology & related works research literature
